## Classification rationale

- **Retain-product:** engineering/research/independent qualification whose acceptance changes what RAHP itself can claim. Independently verified acceptance is not implied by green CI.
- **Registry-transfer-candidate:** a particular external subject/version, remediation authority or case lifecycle dominates. Some items are coordinators and may be closed after canonical handoff instead of transferred. Historical assessments #619/#620 and wrapper #272 require a disposition review before migration.
- **Keep-specialist-pending-review:** external privacy findings should continue to use canonical DPIP ownership; the registry may coordinate referral status but cannot substitute for specialist findings.
- **Mixed-split:** the original scope contains multiple distinct obligations. Retain RAHP/Interop tooling work at its real owner and transfer only the external assessment residual.

## Migration controls and stop decisions

1. **Pilot first:** [registry issue #2](https://github.com/sankarshanmukhopadhyay/rahp-assessment-registry/issues/2). Shadow case #1203 remains authoritative until a properly accepted transfer.
2. **Owner before close:** Do not close an active source-system obligation merely to improve queue statistics. Prove receiving owner, source linkage, recorded obligations, privacy and external evidence constraints.
3. **No outcome promotion:** An upstream merge, dependency release or registry green CI does not establish RAHP PASS or independent human approval.
4. **Do not duplicate authority:** DPIP and Interop Lab retain canonical specialist determination and execution evidence. Registry stores case linkage and governance coordination only.
5. **Automation remains unchanged:** No monitor-to-RAHP issue routing adjustments without an accepted end-to-end pilot; a new repository should not become a second duplicate queue.
6. **Refresh the inventory before acting:** New source changes, issue comments, closures or later intake can invalidate this 2026-11-16 snapshot. It is a finite review input, not a permanently authoritative register.
