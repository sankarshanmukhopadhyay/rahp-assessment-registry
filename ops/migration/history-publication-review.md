# Historical source archive review

The staged historical archive in Registry PR #17 is scanned **without merging or publishing it**. The read-only review workflow checks both compressed and decompressed digests, source record counts, issue identity URLs, decompression bounds and aggregate patterns of possible credentials or contact details.

The review output contains category counts rather than matched human-authored text. A pattern scan is not exhaustive, a privacy determination or authorization to republish third-party content. Manual review for amplification, attribution, sensitive text and redaction remains necessary.

The capture does not include every issue-specific timeline class or external evidence bytes. Therefore integrity validation alone does not satisfy Registry #11's historical completeness gate. No accepted assessment outcomes, authority transfer, operational cutover or release is implied.
