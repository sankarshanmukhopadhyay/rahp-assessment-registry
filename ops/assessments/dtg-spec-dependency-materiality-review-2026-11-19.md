### Composition and falsification requirements

The substantive interaction is not simply “two dependency majors”: it is whether downstream implementations continue to **reject unauthorized content and operations** as their wire/schema/runtime dependencies diverge.

1. Build at least one representative `trust-tasks-https` `jwt` feature consumer against the earlier and new public `jsonwebtoken` types. Verify expected compile failure or migration requirements, then exercise valid token, wrong key, wrong algorithm, expired and wrong-audience refusal, not only build success.
2. Exercise an unrecognized namespace declared `extCritical` through actual Rust, TypeScript, Go and Dart consumption paths. A successful decode that silently drops the namespace is a failure even if shape validation passes.
3. Reproduce the VAC purpose/ACL/grant/memory/room chain with no purpose, multiple authorized purposes, a widened delegated purpose, `modelTraining` and revoked grants. A rejection must be **no-effect and non-disclosing**, not simply a response error after a read.
4. Keep room pagination and requested delivery receipts as separate propositions; a delivery-layer receipt cannot become evidence of application acceptance or delegated authorization.

These are **remaining or downstream-specific falsification requirements**. The upstream Rust `extCritical` and JWT tests above were executed; the VTI integration, other languages, cross-context purpose and production enforcement tests listed here were not. No scope-constrained data collection, disclosure, retention or cross-context correlation fact has yet qualified a new Registry #88 DPIP referral. Toolkit #1194 already holds the source-bound privacy applicability question; [Registry specialist-return contracts](../../scripts/registry_specialist_return.py) explicitly require a uniquely admitted real DPIP examination. Creating a duplicate referral solely because this release contains purpose terminology would manufacture specialist authority. **DPIP applicability for #88: no new referral admitted; follow existing #1194.**
